    Idea For The Map Between Log And Ordinary Stable Map Moduli Space

    idea

    Consider degree \(1\) genus \(0\) stable maps with one marked point to \(\mathbb P^2\) endowed with divisorial log structure coming from \(D \subset \mathbb P^2\) a smooth divisor.
