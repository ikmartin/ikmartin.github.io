    Polytope And Polyhedron

    math

    Definition

    • A \(\mathcal V\)-polytope is the convex hull of a finite set of points \(P\subset \mathbb R^d\).
    • A \(\mathcal H\)-polyhedron is the intersection of finitely many affine half-spaces in \(\mathbb R^d\)
    • An \(\mathcal H\)-polytope is a bounded \(\mathcal H\)-polyhedron
    • A polytope is a set of points \(P\subset \mathbb R^d\) which can be presented either as a \(\mathcal V\)-polytope or a \(\mathcal H\)-polytope.
    • Polytopes are necessarily compact, polyhedra are not.
