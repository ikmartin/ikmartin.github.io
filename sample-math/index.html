    Inline shortcuts

    The affine line \(\bA^1\) sits over \(\Spec \bZ\), and \(\bP^n\) is projective space over a field \(k\). A coherent sheaf \(\cF\) on a scheme \(X\) has stalks \(\cF_x\), and \(\cO_X\) is the structure sheaf. The maximal ideal is \(\frakm\), a prime is \(\frakp\), and \(\ol{X}\) denotes a compactification.

    Operators and groups

    We use operator names like \(\Hom(\cF, \cG)\), \(\Aut(X)\), \(\Pic(X)\), \(\Sym^n V\), \(\QCoh(X)\), and \(\Spec\), plus algebraic groups \(\GL_n\), \(\SL_n\), and \(\PGL_n\). Sheaf Hom is \(\sHom(\cF,\cG)\).

    Display and aligned math

    The moduli space of stable curves is \(\Mgnbar\), a compactification of \(\Mgn\). A basic identity: \[ \chi(\cO_X) = \sum_{i} (-1)^i \dim_k H^i(X, \cO_X). \]

    An aligned computation exercising align* and several macros at once:

    \begin{align*} \Pic(\bP^n) &\cong \bZ, \\ H^\bullet(\bP^n;\bZ) &\cong \bZ[t]/(t^{n+1}), \\ \cO_{\bP^n}(d) &= \cO(1)^{\otimes d}, \qquad d \in \bZ. \end{align*}

    Fraktur, script, and blackboard together: \[ \frakm \subset \cO_{X,x}, \qquad \scrF \in \QCoh(X), \qquad \bR, \bC, \bQ, \bZ, \bN. \]

    Theorem-like environments

    Both the Org form (#+begin_theorem) and the LaTeX form (\begin{theorem}) are unified into styled boxes. proof is special: a collapsible with a vertical rule down the left.

    Definition

    A collection \(\{E_i\}\) in \(D^b(X)\) is exceptional if \(\Hom(E_i, E_i[n]) = k\) when \(n = 0\) and \(0\) otherwise, and \(\Hom(E_i, E_j[n]) = 0\) for all \(n\) when \(i > j\).

    Theorem

    (Pontryagin Duality). For \(G\) a locally compact abelian group, the map \(G \to \hat{\hat G}\), \(g \mapsto \chi(g,-)\), is a natural isomorphism.

    Proof

    Immediate once the evaluation pairing is checked to be continuous and \(\Spec \bZ\)-linear; the details are a diagram chase. \(\square\)

    The LaTeX form renders identically (here a \begin{lemma}):

    Lemma

    Every short exact sequence of \(\cO_X\)-modules induces a long exact sequence in cohomology \(H^\bullet(X, -)\).
